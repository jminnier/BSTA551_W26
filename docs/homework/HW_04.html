Problem 1: Confidence Intervals for Proportions (Devore Section 8.3)

In a pilot study for a new antibiotic, 8 out of 60 patients experienced gastrointestinal side effects.

(a) Compute the sample proportion \(\hat{p}\) and construct a 95% confidence interval using the Wald interval: \(\hat{p} \pm z_{\alpha/2}\sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})}{n}}\)

(b) Compute the 95% Score (Wilson) interval using prop.test(x, n, correct = FALSE). Compare to the Wald interval.

(c) Explain why the Score interval is generally preferred over the Wald interval, especially when \(\hat{p}\) is close to 0 or 1.

(d) The FDA requires that the upper bound of the 95% CI for side effect rate be below 20% for approval. Based on your Score interval, would this drug meet that criterion?
